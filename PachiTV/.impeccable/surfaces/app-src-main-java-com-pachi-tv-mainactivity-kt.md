# Ziota home and its panels (Android TV launcher)

Mode: operate. Audience: the owner, alone, from the couch with a remote, mostly at night.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a calm night-time living-room screen where the time and the apps lead; refuses the category default of a grey glass dock under a glowing neon accent.
OWN-WORLD: near-black ground, white and three greys, one accent per theme used only for "on" states and the focus dot; subtle frosted glass blurred once, radius 16 to 20, Montserrat only, 8 dp spacing; focus turns a surface white.
STORY: the owner reads the time and favorites at a glance, presses up twice for the Control Center (notifications, toggles, Prender PC), opens apps in few presses, and lets music take the screen when idle.
FIRST VIEWPORT: big clock top-left (about 60 sp) over a grey date and weather line, music pill centered, TF mark at 40 percent top-right, rows of glass cards; the focused card turns white with the accent dot top-right.
FORM: candidate 4 (station clock: one huge clock, one accent dot like a second hand) fused with the canon dark-glass card, both chosen by the user; seed:e400527381121dbf
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
